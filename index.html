Frequently Asked Questions

What is the turnaround time for orders?

All 4evercharmed pieces have a standard turnaround time of 1–2 weeks as each order is carefully prepared and quality-checked before dispatch.

How do I pay and confirm my order?

We accept Instant EFT / Bank Transfer. Once you send your order via WhatsApp and complete your payment, tap "Notify Us / Send Proof of Payment" so we can immediately start preparing your pieces.

How does Aramex delivery work?

Once your order is ready, we dispatch nationwide via Aramex door-to-door courier (R99, or FREE on orders over R500). You will receive your tracking details via WhatsApp as soon as it leaves us.

How do I order a custom Bubble Name Necklace?

Click on either the Gold or Silver Bubble Name Necklace to open the product view, type your desired name or initials into the customisation box, and click Add to Bag. Your personalised name will automatically appear on your order summary.
